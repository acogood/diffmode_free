# Marketing Channel Menu — 2026 Edition

**What this is.** A full list of ways to get customers, updated for 2026. It's built for small teams with more time than money — solo founders, first marketing hires, bootstrapped startups. Every channel gets a quick read on cost, how easy it is to track, and whether it's a good fit when you're scrappy.

**How to read it.** Two columns do the heavy lifting:

- **Solo fit** — how much a cash-poor, time-rich founder can get out of it (High / Med / Low).
- **What changed** — what's different since 2025, and why you should care.

Based on a June 2026 research pass. The numbers are point-in-time — treat them as rough, not exact. Double-check before you quote any of them.

---

## What changed since 2025 (the 30-second version)

Three big shifts. Not a new app to chase — deeper than that.

1. **AI now decides what people find.** Most Google searches end with no click — people read the AI answer and move on. So getting named by AI (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google's AI answers) is now its own job, not a footnote under SEO. The clicks that *do* come from AI convert a little better than normal search.
2. **Paid attention got pricier; owned channels got safer.** It costs more to buy a customer than it did a year ago, and ads on LinkedIn and social keep getting more expensive. Email, community, and content cost little and keep paying off. On a tight budget, lean toward the stuff that compounds.
3. **"Social" and "SEO" each split in two.** Brand pages barely reach anyone now — the reach moved to founder faces and short video. And "search" is no longer just blue links; it's links + AI answers + AI-picked feeds.

**Bottom line:** the 2025 list is still about 80% right as an inventory. The fixes: promote AI discovery to its own section, rethink SEO and social, fold voice search into AI, split short video out, add a few new platforms, and tag every channel for solo-founder fit.

## 0. Getting Found by AI  ★ NEW — the big 2026 change

In 2025 this was a single line. Now it's a section. When someone asks ChatGPT or Google's AI a question, you want your product named in the answer — even if they never click. That's a brand win and, sometimes, a traffic win at the same time.

| Channel | Helps with | Cost | Trackable | Solo fit | What changed & why it matters |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Getting cited by AI | Awareness, Conversion | Low–Med | Low | **High** | Get named in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google's AI answers. Often no click — works like free PR. AI tends to trust outside coverage and clear, well-structured pages more than your own marketing. This is just good content work, extended. |
| Visits from AI answers | Conversion | Low | Med | **High** | The clicks you *do* get from AI. Still a small slice of traffic, but they convert a bit better than normal search. Track them on their own so you can watch them grow. |
| AI shopping agents | Awareness, Conversion | Low–Med | Low | Med (new) | Soon, AI assistants will pick tools for people. To get picked: clear pricing, good docs, easy integrations. Early days — worth knowing, not worth obsessing over. |
| Ads inside AI answers | Awareness | High | Med | Low (new) | Paid spots inside AI answers. Still messy and changing fast (Perplexity dropped ads early 2026; Google is adding them). For funded teams experimenting only. |
| AI-picked feeds (Google Discover) | Awareness | Low | Med | Med | Google can surface a fresh article to tens of thousands of people in a day or two. Rewards original, in-depth, brand-new posts. Skips clickbait and reruns. |

> **How to measure it:** you won't see every win as a click. Track how often AI names you, how you stack up against rivals, and whether AI describes you in a good light.

## 2. Short Video  ★ NEW

One 2025 row, now its own section. Short clips are the highest-payoff format for many teams — even in B2B. Best part: make it once, post it everywhere. The apps reward how long people watch and rewatch, not your follower count.

| Channel | Helps with | Cost | Trackable | Solo fit | What changed & why it matters |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| TikTok (and TikTok search) | Awareness, Engagement | Low | Med | Med | A good clip beats a big following. People also search TikTok like Google — your words on screen matter. |
| YouTube Shorts | Awareness, Engagement | Low | Med | Med | Clips under a minute get a push. Old ones can resurface when a topic trends again. |
| Instagram Reels | Awareness, Engagement | Low | Med | Med | Reels carry Instagram's reach now — plain photo posts don't. |
| LinkedIn short video | Awareness, Engagement, Conversion | Low | Med | **High** | Best for B2B — show your product and your face right in front of buyers. |

> **Founder tip:** turn writing into video. Reuse your posts and newsletters as scripts so you barely spend on production.

## 3. Community & Conversation  ★ NEW

Building a community went from "nice extra" to a real way to grow — it brings in customers, keeps them, and gets them to spend more, all at lower cost. The catch: it starts slow, works best once people clearly want your product, and needs real expertise and a clear niche.

| Channel | Helps with | Cost | Trackable | Solo fit | What changed & why it matters |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Your own community (Circle / Slack / Discord) | Engagement, Conversion, Loyalty, Advocacy | Low–Med | Med | Med–High | Start tiny with early fans. Members helping members lowers cost and lifts loyalty. |
| Open communities (Reddit, niche subreddits) | Awareness, Engagement | Low | Low | Med | Rethought. Great for research and real conversations — not for getting cited by AI (Reddit's AI mentions dropped by about half over late 2025). No selling; just help. |
| Bluesky communities ★ NEW | Awareness, Engagement | Low | Low | Med | ~40M+ users. Tight tech, media, and open-source circles. Niche and high-engagement — not mass reach. |
| Slack / Discord groups run by others | Engagement, Conversion | Low | Med | Med | B2B-friendly. Be helpful where your buyers already hang out. |
| Industry forums | Awareness, Engagement | Low | Low | Low | Fading, but still gold in some niches. |

## 7. What to Focus On First (2026)

The most useful page here. What to lean into now vs. save for later, if you're a solo or bootstrapped B2B founder.

| Channel / area | Priority now | Money needed | Time to first signal | Notes |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Buyer-ready content + search / AI visibility | **High** | Low–Med | Medium (3–9 mo) | Your compounding engine. Feeds both search and AI answers. Focus on "ready to buy" pages. |
| Email (newsletter + lifecycle) | **High** | Low | Short (weeks) | Best payback, you own the audience, plays nice with everything. |
| Outbound (cold email + LinkedIn DMs) | **High** | Low | Very short (days–weeks) | Fast conversations. Keep it targeted and respectful. |
| Founder posting (LinkedIn / X / Threads) | **High** | Low | Short–Med | Builds your name and demand. Be consistent. |
| Getting found by AI | **High** | Low–Med | Medium | Extends your content work. Earns mentions and better-converting clicks. |
| Your own community | Med–High | Low–Med | Med–Long | Keeps and grows customers. Best once people clearly want your product. |
| Short video | Medium | Low–Med | Medium | High payoff when you stay consistent. Reuse everywhere. |
| Partners / marketplaces / affiliates | Medium | Low–Med | Med–Long | Warm, high-intent leads at low cost once set up. |
| Paid search — brand name | Medium | Low–Med | Short | Cheap; protects your name. |
| Paid social — cold ads | Low (early) | **High** | Medium | Pricey and hard to track now. Later-stage or narrow targeting only. |
| Display / programmatic | Low | **High** | Medium | Low payoff. Brand-building at scale only. |
| Podcast ads | Low | **High** | Medium | Works, but costly. Later-stage. |

> **Compounding vs. one-and-done:** content, AI visibility, email lists, community, and product loops build up over time. Most ads and posts reset the moment you stop paying or posting. On a tight budget, bet on what builds up.

## 8. What changed vs. the 2025 list

**Added** — Getting Found by AI (its own section); Short Video; Community & Conversation; newsletter platforms; RCS and WhatsApp messaging; Threads and Bluesky; outbound (cold email / LinkedIn DMs) as a group; a "solo fit" tag on every channel; and the focus list (Section 7).

**Dropped / folded in** — Voice search is gone as its own thing (it's just talking to AI now → Section 0). The old "AI answer optimization" line got promoted out of Content into Section 0. Community-building moved to Section 3.

**Rethought** — SEO is now "Search & answer visibility"; organic social split into founder-led (strong) vs. brand page (weak); Reddit is for community and research, not AI mentions; paid search split into brand vs. generic; display and programmatic downgraded for small teams; partnerships and marketplace listings promoted to first-class; press flagged as fuel for AI visibility; influencers reframed as small creators + real customer content.

**Unchanged** — billboards, print, most TV / radio / streaming, and the whole Unusual & Hard-to-Copy list (human-authenticity plays — if anything, more valuable now).

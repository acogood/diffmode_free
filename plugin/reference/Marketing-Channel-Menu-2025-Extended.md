## **Unconventional & Non-Scalable Channels**

| **Micro-Community** | Private Facebook groups | Engagement, Conversion | Low | Medium | Access-gated, relationship-dependent |
| **Micro-Community** | Industry-specific forums | Awareness, Engagement | Low | Low | Declining but still valuable in certain niches |
